Privacy policy

Last updated October 2026

The CIG Executive Hub is the internal operations tool for Culinary Innovations Group's leadership and management teams, on the web and as an iPhone app. It is not open to the public: every account is created by a CIG administrator. This page explains what the Hub keeps, why, and who can see it.

What the Hub keeps

  • Your account — your name, work email, job title, and which restaurants, locations and sections you can use.
  • What you put in — posts, comments and likes, messages and attachments, calendar entries, checklist updates, documents sent for signature, availability and time off requests, expense receipts (a photo or PDF, the amount, where and why), and device, event and help requests.
  • Catering enquiries — the contact and event details a guest sends when their enquiry is forwarded to the Hub.
  • Notifications — what you were sent and whether you have read it, so the Hub knows what is still waiting on you.
  • On the iPhone app — a notification token, only if you allow notifications.

The Hub does not collect payment card details, your location, your contacts, or anything for advertising.

How it is used

Only to run the Hub: signing you in, showing each person what their role needs, sending the notifications and emails they have asked for, and producing the reports CIG's teams work from.

Who can see it

People in the Hub see what their role and locations need — a manager sees their own location, finance sees expenses, and so on. CIG administrators can see the whole Hub so they can fix problems, except private conversations they have not been added to.

Where it is stored

The Hub runs on Google Firebase (sign-in, database and file storage) and Google Workspace (Drive, and the mailbox catering enquiries are forwarded to). Emails are sent through Resend. Information is not sold, rented or shared with anyone for marketing.

How long it is kept

  • Receipt photos and expense reports: ninety days. Receipt records are kept as company financial records.
  • Your account: for as long as you work with CIG. When someone leaves, their access is deactivated immediately — in the Hub, in CIG's back-of-house software and applications, and in their company email.

Your choices

You choose which emails you get under Notifications in your profile. To correct or remove your information, email info@cigconcepts.com. You can also delete your account from your profile.

Children

The Hub is for adult employees of Culinary Innovations Group and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your information: info@cigconcepts.com.
